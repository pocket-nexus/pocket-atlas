A reconstruction of Nathan Road at the original Chungking Mansions frontage,
with a view into the shopping arcade and the service lightwell. It uses Atlas's
shared outdoor PBR, twilight sky, post, batching and portable motion contracts.
The period is 1990; this is an interpretation supported by bracketing photographs,
not a claim that a camera photographed this exact view, lighting or tenant roster
in 1990.

## Historical evidence

- The Hong Kong government's [20 November 2012 district visit](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/201211/20/P201211200507.htm)
  dates the building to 1961.
- [Nathan Road, 1 October 1981](https://gwulo.com/media/41308), photographed by
  m20wc51, establishes the original horizontal facade, adjoining Imperial Hotel,
  carriageway, pedestrian rails and projecting sign hierarchy before 1990.
- [Nathan Road, 28 August 1994](https://gwulo.com/media/38539), photographed by
  Yvon from Ottawa, supplies a close later comparison for layered, oversized
  signboards, traffic and lower commercial windows. It is four years after the
  requested date; its individual advertisers are not copied into 1990.
- [John Lambon's 2005 photograph](https://gwulo.com/media/46437), reproduced by
  permission of the photographer and HKU Press, supplies pre-renovation detail.
  It is reference material only; no photo pixels are shipped.
- Eunice Seng's [*The City in a Building*](https://hub.hku.hk/bitstream/10722/244763/1/content.pdf),
  pp. 86, 91–96, describes the composite building and documents the 2011 exterior
  cleanup. That later unified facade, glass retail skin and LED signage are
  excluded. The paper is historical scholarship, not a 1990 measured survey.
- Current [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/39315991) geometry
  gives the retained five-block arrangement. `world/massing.ts` records the
  five source way IDs and rounded metre coordinates, © OpenStreetMap contributors,
  [ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The 1990 persistence of these
  outlines is inferred from the old photographs and historical account.
- [Toyota's official vehicle chronology](https://www.toyota-global.com/company/history_of_toyota/75years/common/pdf/Vehicle_Lineage_Chart_en.pdf)
  places the Crown Comfort in 1995; the taxi is therefore an earlier angular
  red/silver sedan silhouette. It does not claim a particular registration or
  exact Crown trim. The bus is a photo-derived 9.7 m two-axle MCW-era form,
  informed by [Gakei's fleet photographs](https://www.gakei.hk/kmw/kmw.htm),
  without an invented route assignment.

The ignored `.pocket-build/research/hk-chungking-mansions-1990/` directory holds
14 photographs and `manifest.tsv` with capture dates, authors, licences, URLs and
limits; modern OSM XML; an explicit 20 m grid context map; colour sample receipts;
and `REPORT.md`. The purported 1989 Dick Leonhardt photograph is marked disputed
because its Gwulo discussion questions its date. It does not establish any
exact-year tenant.

## Authored reconstruction and limits

The origin is the approximate west threshold (22.29646° N, 114.172402° E),
+X east, −Z north, metres. The retained Block A outline is approximately 36.4 m
north–south; the whole modern five-block site is approximately 92.6 × 64.2 m.
The 55 m top, 3.15 m upper floor pitch, street width, shop dimensions and relief
are photo estimates. Frontage and road are straightened locally; this does not
reproduce the slight surveyed Nathan Road bearing change.

Exterior boards with the building's name identify the real place. Other signs
say only ordinary trade categories: guest houses, exchange, tailoring, watches,
electronics and food. Their lettering, colours and positions reconstruct the
period's visual density; they are not assertions that a named firm traded there
in 1990. Arcade cabinets, stocked shelves, lift doors, service fixtures and the
accessible lightwell view are conjectural spatial detail. There is no claim that
the lightwell was publicly accessible. Far neighbour facades are simplified.

Humid civil twilight uses an authored sun direction of azimuth 265°, elevation
−5° with direct sunlight disabled. It is a lighting study, not a recovered
weather observation. Window lights and shop lamps are illustrative. Reference
photos retain film/scan colour casts, so their sampled sRGB values are not
absolute reflectance measurements.

The 120 s loop contains a northbound bus on the west carriageway, a southbound
taxi on the east, and three ordinary people. Vehicle wrap points lie at least
450 m away in full fog. All figure gait and breathing phases repeat over the
same loop. No film character, fan merchandise, post-1990 car, contemporary
shopping mall or video billboard appears.

The six shots are Nathan Road, Mansions, Signs, Entrance, Arcade and Lightwell.
